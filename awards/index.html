Distinguished Service Award

The purpose of this award is to celebrate those CP community members who have made exceptional contributions to support the CP community. Areas of support could include, but are not limited to: association service, service as an editor, conference organisation, representation of CP in other organizations, etc., resulting in positive effects on the research field. Doctoral Research Award

The ACP Doctoral Research Award is awarded every year to a promising young researcher working in the area of constraint programming and who defended their thesis between January and December of the previous two years. The award, in the form of a certificate, is presented at the CP conference, where the winning researcher will be invited to give a talk about their PhD thesis. The award includes a cash prize of 500 euros, plus free registration for the conference. ...

Early Career Researcher Award

The ACP Early Career Researcher Award is awarded every year to a researcher who has completed their PhD at least 2 years and at most 8 years before the year of the competition, with a scientific track record showing great promise in the area of Constraint Programming. The award was established in 2021 after a proposal by Prof. Eugene Freuder to the ACP. Prof. Freuder has provided seed funding for the award by the donation of the monetary portion of his 2020 IJCAI Research Excellence Award to the ACP. ...
