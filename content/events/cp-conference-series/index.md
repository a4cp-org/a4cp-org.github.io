### Significant antecedents of the CP conferences and workshops

* [International Conf. on Constraints in Computational Logics](http://www.informatik.uni-trier.de/~ley/db/conf/ccl/ccl94.html), Munich, 1994
* Chatillon Spring School on Constraints, Chatillon-sur-Seine, France, 1994
* [Workshops on Constraint Logic Programming](http://www.informatik.uni-trier.de/~ley/db/conf/wclp/wclp91.html), Marseille, 1991, 1992 & 1993
* AAAI-91 Spring Symposium on Constraint-Based Reasoning, Stanford U.
* AAAI-90 Workshop on Constraint Directed Reasoning, Boston
* IJCAI-89 Workshop on Constraint Processing, Detroit
* Workshop on Constraint Logic Programming, Alton Jones, 1988
