## Primary conferences

* The International Conference on Principles and Practice of Constraint Programming (CP) is a central publishing and meeting place for CP research [CP Conference series page](/events/cp-conference-series)
* Another venue that has CP at its core is the International Conference on the Integration of Constraint Programming, Artificial Intelligence, and Operations Research [CPAIOR Conference series page](https://cpaior.org)

## Primary journal

* The [Constraints journal](https://www.springer.com/journal/10601) provides a common forum for the many disciplines interested in constraint programming and constraint satisfaction and optimization, and the many application domains in which constraint technology is employed.

  + See our own [Constraints journal](/constraints-journal/cj2) page.

## Artificial Intelligence venues

CP is one of the many subfields of AI, and hence CP research is also often published in all major AI journals and conferences, such as:

* International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence ([IJCAI](https://www.ijcai.org))
* AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence ([AAAI](https://aaai.org))
* European Conference on Artificial Intelligence ([ECAI](https://www.eurai.org))
* International Conference on Logic Programming ([ICLP](https://www.cs.nmsu.edu/ALP/conferences/))
